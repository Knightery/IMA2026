### Company & Objective Overview
* **Company:** West Valley Fresh Distribution Company is a produce intermediary based in Southern California.
* **Goal:** The CEO is considering entering the organic Hass avocado market and needs a profitability and sensitivity analysis to make a decision.

### Key Figures for Profitability & Logistics
* **Available Supply:** The company can purchase 20,000 organic Hass avocados from local farms.
* **Acquisition Cost:** The cost to purchase these avocados is $.20 each.
* **Transportation Costs:** Shipping costs $50 for every 100 miles, plus a fixed cost of $1,500.
* **Pricing Strategy:** The planned wholesale price is 40% of the local retail price.
* **Logistics Sensitivity:** The analysis must calculate scenarios where shipping and logistics expenses increase by 50% and decrease by 50%.

### Market Data Context
* **Data Source:** Sales data was sourced from the Hass Avocado Board (HAB).
* **Data Scope:** The data covers 41 metropolitan regions in the U.S.
* **Timeframes:** The downloaded data is from January 2019 to November 2022, but the assignment questions require analysis from January 2019 to June 2025.
* **Market Share:** Approximately 90% of Hass avocados in the U.S. are imported from Mexico, with the remaining 10% grown in Southern California.
* **Analytical Requirements:** The analysis requires exploring price impacts on sales volume, seasonality, and profit margins.
* **Supplementary Data:** The case requires downloading an external "SALES DATA" file to complete the assignment.
