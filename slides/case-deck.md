# A selective 5-city pilot is the strongest entry path for the 20,000-unit organic order

<div class="kpi-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Recommendation</h3>
    <div class="metric success">5-city launch</div>
    <p class="small">Seattle, Boise, Portland, Spokane, and San Diego maximize expected profit under the June 2026 pricing assumption.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Base-case economics</h3>
    <div class="metric">$13.7k avg profit</div>
    <p class="small">The selected cities pair premium June pricing with manageable miles, producing a top-five average margin of 69.8%.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Why it is resilient</h3>
    <div class="metric compare">Same 5 cities</div>
    <p class="small">The recommendation does not change when shipping costs move up 50% or down 50%.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="small muted">Model note: each city is scored as a comparable 20,000-unit scenario using June 2025 retail price as the June 2026 proxy.</p>

# Seasonality creates a timing gap between volume and price

<div class="columns equal">
  <div class="col">

![w:100%](../outputs/q8_seasonality_volume.png)

<p class="caption">Volume arrives early. Organic monthly volume peaks in <strong>January</strong>, conventional peaks in <strong>May</strong>, and both soften into <strong>November</strong>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="col">

![w:100%](../outputs/q8_seasonality_price.png)

<p class="caption">Price crests later. Both organic and conventional monthly prices peak in <strong>July</strong> and bottom in <strong>February</strong>.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="small muted">The market absorbs more units before it offers peak pricing, so timing affects margin capture, promotions, and inventory allocation.</p>

# The pilot screen should be price support, organic readiness, and route efficiency

<div class="columns equal">
  <div class="col">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Price support</h3>
      <div class="metric success">$2.09 to $2.66</div>
      <p class="small">The five selected cities are also the five highest-price June 2025 organic markets in the model.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Organic readiness</h3>
      <div class="metric compare">Seattle leads 5 of 7</div>
      <p class="small">Organic adoption is uneven by city, so total sales scale alone is not enough to pick the best launch markets.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="col">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Route efficiency</h3>
      <div class="metric">$813 to $1,024</div>
      <p class="small">Even after a 50% freight increase, the recommended cities lose only about $0.8k to $1.0k each and keep the same ranking.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="small muted">This deck tests those three filters against sales leadership, organic mix, profitability, freight sensitivity, and seasonality.</p>
