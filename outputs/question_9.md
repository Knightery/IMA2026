# Question 9

## Recommendations to Manage Seasonality
- Use **contract tiers** with growers and retailers (base volume + seasonal flex volume) so supply can expand in high-demand months without overcommitting year-round.
- Shift to **seasonal pricing and promotion calendars**: promote aggressively in low-volume months and protect margin when demand is naturally high.
- Build a **city-level allocation model** that routes supply toward markets with the highest expected margin each month.
- Improve **cold-chain and transit reliability** in peak months to reduce spoilage and stockouts when velocity is highest.
- Expand **demand smoothing channels** (food-service contracts, subscription boxes, institutional buyers) to absorb excess in weaker months.
- Coordinate with trade associations on **consumer education campaigns** (recipes, health messaging) timed to historically soft months.
- Run monthly **S&OP reviews** (forecast vs actual price/volume) and adjust purchase commitments and distribution plans quickly.
